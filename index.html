538,000 Sq Ft Total Area

Multi-Stage Quality Management System

Global brands demand consistency in every single unit. Maven enforces strict multi-phase quality controls throughout production:

  • ✔ Incoming Raw Material Audit: Inspecting glass batch purity and paper stock integrity.
  • ✔ In-Process Automated Inspection: Continuous dimensional and wall-thickness monitoring.
  • ✔ Leakage & Compatibility Testing: Vacuum chamber sealing verification for bottle-pump fits.
  • ✔ Pre-Shipment Quality Control: Final visual inspection prior to export packaging.
