OUR HERITAGE

42 YEARS OF MANUFACTURING HERITAGE

“Backed by Meitong Group’s manufacturing heritage dating to 1984, Zhejiang Maven Industrial Technology Co., Ltd. delivers integrated primary and secondary packaging solutions to global brands.”

Zhejiang Maven Industrial Technology Co., Ltd. operates as the dedicated international B2B packaging division of Meitong Group. Grounded in over four decades of industrial manufacturing heritage since 1984, Meitong Group built extensive production leadership across high-clarity glass melting, automated bottle manufacturing, plastic injection molding, and luxury rigid paper box fabrication.

Operating a state-of-the-art 538,000 sq ft manufacturing facility in Tongxiang, Zhejiang, alongside direct international support via MAVEN US LLC in Miami, Florida, Maven provides global prestige beauty, fragrance, skincare, and nutraceutical brands with seamless single-source packaging solutions, technical CAD mold development, and complete supply chain transparency.
