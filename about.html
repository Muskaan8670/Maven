FOUR DECADES OF PACKAGING HERITAGE

Zhejiang Maven Industrial Technology Co., Ltd. was established in 1984 in Tongxiang, Zhejiang, China, situated in the industrial center of Jiangsu, Zhejiang, and Shanghai. For over four decades, Maven has dedicated its core focus to packaging container development, glass forming, and luxury box manufacturing.

In 2019, Maven became an integral subsidiary of Meitong Group (MEITONG GROUP), enhancing our research capabilities, factory automation, and international service network.

Core Corporate Ideology

Committed to producing products perfectly. We aim to provide original product design, structural concept development, and sustainable packaging solutions for our global brand partners.
